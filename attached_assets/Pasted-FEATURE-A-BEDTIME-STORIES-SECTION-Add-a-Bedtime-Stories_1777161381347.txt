FEATURE A — BEDTIME STORIES SECTION
Add a “Bedtime Stories” section to the app. This is a library of short audio stories for children, read in a warm and calm human voice — not AI generated voice. The stories play like audiobooks with illustrated screens.
Add a new button on the home screen and bottom navigation bar called “Stories 📖” / “قصص 📖”. This opens the stories library screen.
STORIES LIBRARY SCREEN: Show a cozy nighttime themed screen with a dark blue/purple gradient background and stars. Title says “Bedtime Stories 🌙” / “قصص قبل النوم 🌙”. Show stories as cards in a vertical list. Each card shows: story illustration image, story title in Arabic and English, duration in minutes, age range, and a play button. Add a filter bar at top: All / Arabic / English / 3-6 years / 7-10 years.
STORY PLAYER SCREEN: When child taps a story, open a full screen player with a large cozy illustration that changes every 30–60 seconds matching the part of the story being told, story title at top, large play/pause button in center, progress bar showing how far through the story, current time and total time, a sleepy version of Adam or Lulu in the corner with eyes slowly closing animated to look like they are falling asleep listening to the story, auto-dim screen after 2 minutes of playing, sleep timer option where story stops after 15, 30, or 60 minutes. The audio files are pre-recorded human voice MP3 files stored in the app — NOT generated by any AI or TTS API. This means zero API cost for stories.
ARABIC STORIES:
Story 1 — الأسد الصغير الشجاع: Duration 5 minutes, Age 4–8. A little lion is scared of the dark forest but discovers that being brave means doing something even when you are scared. He helps a lost bird find its way home and realizes his heart is bigger than his fear. Warm gentle ending where he falls asleep under the stars feeling proud. Tone: warm, slow, calm, pause after each sentence, soft wind and bird and cricket sound effects.
Story 2 — نجمة المطر: Duration 6 minutes, Age 4–8. A little star wishes she could help the dry earth below. She cries tears of rain that make flowers grow and children laugh. She learns her tears are her superpower. Tone: dreamy and magical, soft rain sounds throughout.
Story 3 — الفيل الذي نسي: Duration 7 minutes, Age 5–9. Little Filo the elephant keeps forgetting things and feels sad until his grandmother says the most important things are kept in the heart not the mind. Funny and heartwarming. Tone: playful at first then warm and tender.
Story 4 — الولد الذي زرع قمراً: Duration 8 minutes, Age 6–10. A boy plants a glowing seed, everyone laughs, but he waters it every night and talks to it kindly. One night a small moon grows and lights the whole village. About patience and believing in yourself. Tone: magical and poetic, very slow and peaceful.
Story 5 — سر المكتبة: Duration 6 minutes, Age 7–11. A girl who hates reading gets locked in a library and the books come alive showing her adventures. By morning she has traveled to 5 worlds. Tone: exciting at first then warm, speed picks up during adventure parts.
ENGLISH STORIES:
Story 6 — The Cloud Who Was Different: Duration 5 minutes, Age 4–8. A cloud who can only make snowflakes in summer is laughed at until children discover his snowflakes on a hot day and dance with joy. About being different. Tone: gentle and whimsical, soft wind and children laughing.
Story 7 — The Lighthouse Keeper’s Cat: Duration 6 minutes, Age 5–9. A cat afraid of water overcomes her fear to save a ship on a stormy night. About courage and love. Tone: cozy and adventurous, storm sounds during climax then calm.
Story 8 — The Boy Who Collected Sunsets: Duration 7 minutes, Age 6–10. A boy draws every sunset and is told it is a waste of time until a famous artist sees his collection and says he captured what no camera could. Tone: slow, poetic, and warm throughout.
Story 9 — The Giant Who Was Lonely: Duration 6 minutes, Age 4–8. A giant everyone fears becomes friends with one little girl who waves at him every day. The village learns what they feared was just lonely. Tone: tender and emotional, simple language.
Story 10 — The Last Cookie: Duration 5 minutes, Age 4–7. Two siblings spend all day trying to trick each other for the last cookie, fall asleep exhausted, and their mom eats it. Tone: playful and funny, light and quick pace.
TECHNICAL FOR STORIES: Store all story audio files as MP3 in the public assets folder — no API call needed to play them. Load story illustrations as static images — one image per story for the card and 3–4 scene images per story that change during playback at pre-set timestamps. Track in Supabase which stories each child has listened to, how many times, and whether they finished. Show a “Listened ✓” badge on completed stories. Add stories to the parent dashboard so parents can see which stories their child listened to and when. If child finishes all 10 stories show a special certificate “Master Storyteller 🌟” signed by Adam. Add a “Tonight’s Story 🌙” card on the home screen that appears after 7:00 PM only, shows the recommended story for tonight based on child’s age, tapping it opens the story player directly.
